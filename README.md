## Weekly Tasks & Deliverables

### Week 1: Data Science Orientation & Problem Definition
- **Submission Date:** September 30, 2026[cite: 1]
- **Domain:** Beauty, Skincare & Wellness Sector[cite: 1]
- **Project Title:** Identifying Early Churn Indicators and Repeat Purchase Drivers in D2C Skincare[cite: 1]
- **Deliverable:** `Week-01/Yuva_Internship_Week1_Report.docx`[cite: 1]

#### Business Problem Summary
- A mid-sized D2C skincare brand experiences a 55% to 60% customer drop-off rate within 90 days after the initial purchase[cite: 1].
- The primary objective is to build an early-warning predictive system to flag at-risk customers within 30 to 45 days post-delivery, targeting a 10% to 15% reduction in 90-day churn[cite: 1].

#### Planned Methodology
1. **Data Ingestion & Quality Audit:** Handle missing survey ratings and audit transaction logs using pandas[cite: 1].
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Cohort analysis, product category breakdown, and discount dependency curves[cite: 1].
3. **Feature Engineering:** RFM calculation, routine bundle indicator (`is_bundle_buyer`), and categorical encoding[cite: 1].
4. **Predictive Modeling:** Benchmark Logistic Regression, followed by Random Forest and XGBoost classifiers with SMOTE for class balance[cite: 1].
5. **Evaluation & CRM Integration:** Prioritize Recall and ROC-AUC metrics to power automated customer replenishment triggers[cite: 1].
